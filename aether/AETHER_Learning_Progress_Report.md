This report details the current progress of the AETHER AI frameworks, **Stratify** and **Principia**, within the rigorous K-12 and Higher Education learning curriculum. The system was designed to build a strong foundation in mathematics and physics, with a focus on genuine understanding, character development, and the potential for novel discoveries.

- **No genuinely new mathematical discoveries** have been made at the K-5 grade levels.
- Both frameworks demonstrate a **strong grasp of fundamental concepts** and clear reasoning.
